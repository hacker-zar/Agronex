# Landing image sources

- `public/landing/nexudrive-product-devices.webp` combines the Agronex/NexuDrive screenshots supplied by the project owner with device frames from [Mobile FIRST MacBook Neo](https://www.webmobilefirst.com/en/mockups/apple-macbook-neo-2026/) and [Mobile FIRST iPhone 18 Pro](https://www.webmobilefirst.com/en/mockups/apple-iphone-18-pro-2026/).
- The provider's pages state that the frames may be modified and used commercially without attribution. The final composite is used as a landing page image; the standalone frames are not included in the repository.
- `public/landing/nexudrive-publicacion.jpg` is a compressed version of the NexuDrive screenshot supplied by the project owner.
